## Objective & Setup

MyDocStatus was created to test search intent clustering around document tracking and verification keywords. Instead of relying on paid acquisition, the focus is 100% on technical on-page SEO and structured schema.

## Ongoing Observations

1. **Search Console Growth:** Clicks have scaled to 80–100 per day with over 5,000 daily search impressions.
2. **On-Page Refinements:** Continuously improving meta titles, content structure, and internal linking to rank for secondary and long-tail keywords.
3. **Monetization Milestone:** Testing traffic consistency across multiple weeks to establish the baseline needed for Google AdSense submission.
