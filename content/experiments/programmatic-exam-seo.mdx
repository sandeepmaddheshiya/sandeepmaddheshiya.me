## Background & Rationale

Generic utility keywords like `photo resizer` have high domain authority competition. To bootstrap traffic on a new domain (`photoresizer.in`), I hypothesized that users searching for specific exam application requirements (e.g. `DSSSB photo size converter`) had high urgency and lower competition.

## Observations

1. **Bing Search Indexing:** Bing indexed and ranked the programmatic exam pages within 10 days of deployment.
2. **The Post-Deadline Cliff:** Once the exam application deadline passed, daily impressions for that specific cluster dropped by over 75%.
3. **Strategic Pivot:** Shifted from purely event-driven exam pages to recurring evergreen dimensional clusters, which later fueled the September 2026 Google ranking breakout.
