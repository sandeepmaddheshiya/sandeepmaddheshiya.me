## Background & Rationale

After accidentally purchasing `photoresizer.us` in early 2026, I built a prototype US passport and visa photo resizer. Traffic began arriving from US immigration queries, and on April 4–5, users clicked "Download & Pay". However, every single payment attempt threw an unhandled gateway error.

## Investigation & Root Cause

1. **Gateway Restriction:** By default, domestic Razorpay merchant accounts reject foreign-issued credit and debit cards unless international acceptance is explicitly approved and enabled with KYC compliance.
2. **Currency Settlement:** Charging US dollars without active multi-currency settlement caused US banking institutions to flag transactions as unrecognized domestic charges.
3. **Lack of User Feedback:** When an international card failed, the frontend displayed a generic error rather than suggesting alternate payment routes.

## The Fix & Result

- Submitted cross-border export documentation to Razorpay on April 5 and obtained international payment approval on April 6.
- Added live webhook handlers to verify transaction status before rendering high-resolution printable 4x6 inch templates.
- **Result:** Within hours of enabling the fix, the first live $5 transaction completed successfully on April 6, 2026.
