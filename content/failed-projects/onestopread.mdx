## Post-Mortem Details

### Phase 1: The Trending Blog Trap (April – May 2026)
- **Domain:** `onestopread.com`
- **Goal:** Launch a broad editorial and trending blog.
- **Outcome:** 0–1 clicks per day. Competing directly with entrenched digital publications on trending keywords with a zero-authority domain was ineffective and consumed 2 months of effort.

### Phase 2: The AI & Cron Exam Automation Pivot (June – July 2026)
- **Concept:** Rebuilt the site into a modern, ad-free alternative to legacy government exam portals (like SarkariExam).
- **Architecture:**
  1. Automated scrapers monitored official government recruitment portals for fresh notification PDFs.
  2. Integrated **Gemini Flash** via backend cron jobs to extract exam dates, eligibility criteria, and fee structures into structured JSON.
  3. Programmatically generated SEO titles, meta descriptions, and keyword clusters.
- **Early Signals:** Reached 5–30 daily clicks across Bing and Google within the first 3 weeks.

### Phase 3: The Algorithmic Cliff (August 2026)
- Around day 25, search engines de-indexed/demoted the programmatic pages.
- Daily clicks dropped from ~30 to 0; daily search impressions collapsed from hundreds to 5–20.
- **Mitigation Attempt:** Shifted from purely automated scrapers to manual verification, including official source citations and links to government portals. Rankings did not recover.
- **Decision:** Dropped the project and redirected engineering focus to utility tools with client-side processing ([PhotoResizer](/projects/photoresizer) and [VisaReadyNow](/projects/visareadynow)).
