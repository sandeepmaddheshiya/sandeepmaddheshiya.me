## Post-Mortem Details

### 1. The Domain Flipping Attempt
- **Domain:** `onestopwrite.com`
- **Initial Plan:** Acquire the domain and list it for sale on domain marketplaces.
- **Result:** No buyer inquiries or secondary market liquidity.

### 2. The Product Pivot
- Rather than leaving the domain idle, I built a clean document and policy generator web utility:
  - Privacy Policy Generator
  - Terms & Conditions Generator
  - Cookie Policy Generator
  - Refund Policy Generator
  - Formal Business & Company Letter Generators

### 3. Monetization & Growth Experiments
- **Phase A (Pay-Per-Use):** Attempted charging a nominal fee per document download. Result: 0 transactions.
- **Phase B (Free Tool):** Made all generators completely free with instant copy/download. Daily traffic hovered between **3–8 active users**.
- **Phase C (SEO, AEO & GEO):** Experimented with structured schema markup, question-and-answer formats for AI search engines (AEO), and generative engine indexing (GEO). Search visibility failed to break through entrenched incumbent rankings.

### 4. Conclusion & Retirement
- Decided not to renew the domain upon expiration, opting instead to direct engineering bandwidth to tools with distinct search wedges like [PhotoResizer](/projects/photoresizer).
