## Project Timeline & Architecture

### Phase 1: Early Conception (Early 2025)
- Began development while working as a full-time software developer at Collegedunia.
- Limited evening and weekend hours slowed early progress and technical research.

### Phase 2: Full Build & AI Acceleration (Late 2025 – Early 2026)
- Accelerated development using LLMs (ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude) and the Antigravity development environment.
- Built a multi-feature suite:
  1. **JD-Targeted Optimizer:** Tailored resume bullet points against specific job description keywords to improve ATS shortlisting probability.
  2. **AI Cover Letter & Interview Prep:** Generated contextual cover letters and practice interview questions from uploaded CVs.
  3. **Template Library:** Integrated 50–80+ structured templates, highlighting 8–10 top corporate-tailored formats.
- Deployed on `zyoraai.com` with paywalls for premium PDF exports and advanced JD tailoring.

### Phase 3: Experimentation & Market Reality (Early – Mid 2026)
- Over 2–4 months of testing, organic discovery was insufficient against entrenched competitors with high domain authority.
- Paid conversion remained flat despite free trial usage.
- **Decision:** Terminated the resume builder direction and pivoted the `zyoraai.com` domain to AI Interior Design.
