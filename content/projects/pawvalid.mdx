## What is it?

**PawValid** ([pawvalid.online](https://pawvalid.online)) is an automated pet travel compliance tool designed to help pet owners navigate complex international veterinary health certificates, rabies titer testing timelines, microchip standards (ISO 11784/11785), and quarantine regulations across global immigration corridors.

## Why did I build it?

While conducting regulatory research for [VisaReadyNow](/projects/visareadynow), I observed a parallel, high-friction compliance problem in international pet relocation:

1. **Fragmented Bureaucracy:** Every country enforces distinct veterinary protocols (USDA APHIS in the US, CFIA in Canada, DEFRA in the UK, and EU Pet Travel Schemes).
2. **Timing Risks:** Rabies neutralizing antibody titer tests (RNATT) must often be completed 30 to 180 days in advance, leading to costly airport rejections or quarantine if timeline calculations are off by even a few days.
3. **High Intent Demand:** Pet owners actively search for clear, reliable verification checklists before booking international flights.

## Current Focus & Architecture

- **Rules Engine:** Structuring bilateral pet import and export rules (vaccination windows, endorsement requirements, tapeworm treatment timings).
- **UI/UX Optimization:** Designing a step-by-step route checker where owners input origin, destination, and departure dates to get a tailored compliance timeline.
- **SEO & Distribution:** Building programmatic travel corridor landing pages to capture high-intent pet immigration searches organically.
