## What is it?

**PhotoResizer** comprises two interconnected projects:
1. **photoresizer.in:** A free client-side image utility to resize, crop, and compress images to strict byte and dimension requirements (e.g., `< 50KB`, `200x230 px`) directly in-browser.
2. **photoresizer.us:** A paid US passport and visa photo compliance tool that validates biometric proportions and replaces backgrounds for official US visa/passport forms.

## The Origin: An Accidental .us Purchase

While registering `photoresizer.in` in early 2026, I followed a tutorial for a promotional .us domain on Namecheap. Due to a missing coupon input on the final screen, I accidentally bought `photoresizer.us` without a discount.

Instead of abandoning the domain, I investigated ways to monetize it. Competitor research into VisaFoto and PhotoGov revealed enormous US search demand for compliant passport photos. I spent months analyzing US State Department photo specifications and built a specialized US visa/passport photo tool on `photoresizer.us`.

## Monetization & The April Payment Loss

- **April 4–5, 2026:** The US tool went live. Early traffic attempted to purchase photos, but transactions failed because international currency settlement was disabled in Razorpay.
- **April 6, 2026:** Razorpay approved international card acceptance, and I secured my **first paying customer**. The prototype went on to generate **$100+ USD** in early sales before full-time job commitments at Collegedunia slowed development.
- **Evolution to VisaReadyNow:** Validating customer willingness to pay on `photoresizer.us` directly prompted the creation of [VisaReadyNow](/projects/visareadynow) to support 190+ countries globally.

## SEO & Growth Strategy on photoresizer.in

### Phase 1: Bing Traction (Feb – May 2026)
Initial indexing in Bing Webmaster Tools yielded 100–200 daily organic visitors, confirming demand for fast client-side image resizing.

### Phase 2: Programmatic Exam Pages & Seasonality (May – Aug 2026)
I launched targeted specification pages for Indian government exams (such as `DSSSB exam photo resizer`). Daily traffic quickly climbed to **400–600 users/day** before dropping back to baseline once application deadlines closed.

### Phase 3: Evergreen Long-Tail Clustering (Aug – Sept 2026)
To escape the seasonality trap, I restructured the keyword hierarchy around recurring, evergreen dimension queries and general file size limits.

### Phase 4: Google Breakout (September 25, 2026)
Google Analytics fired an anomaly detection alert for sudden traffic surges. Search Console confirmed:
- **1,000+ organic clicks/day**
- **25,000+ daily search impressions**
- **Top 5 search ranking** on Google for competitive photo resizing queries.

## Current Experiments

Following my departure from Collegedunia on July 31, 2026, I completed a major UI/UX and SEO overhaul on `photoresizer.us` to mirror the cleaner design of VisaReadyNow, currently generating 100–200 daily impressions on Bing as rankings rebuild.
