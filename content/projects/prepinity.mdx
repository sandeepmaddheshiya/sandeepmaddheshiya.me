## What is it?

**Prepinity** ([prepinity.com](https://prepinity.com)) is an experimental educational resource and exam preparation portal structured to attract high-intent global organic search traffic across test prep and curriculum topics.

## Why did I build it?

After analyzing search CPC differentials and organic query volumes across international markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia), I launched **Prepinity** to test whether a modern, fast-loading educational portal could rank for high-value educational queries without massive editorial overhead.

## Current Status & Traction

- **Organic Search:** Receiving **2–4 clicks per day** from international search engines as new pages get discovered and indexed.
- **Monetization Roadmap:** Planned for display ad monetization (Google AdSense and programmatic ad networks) once traffic scales to consistent daily volume.
- **SEO Experiment:** Testing programmatic content scaffolding, schema markup, and long-tail syllabus query clustering.

## What I'm Learning

- International educational search keywords have high query volume but demand comprehensive topic depth to outrank established educational publishers.
- Iterating on content architecture and fast Core Web Vitals to observe search crawler behavior across multi-tier geographic queries.
