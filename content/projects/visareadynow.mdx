## What is it?

**VisaReadyNow** ([visareadynow.com](https://visareadynow.com)) is a global micro-SaaS that automatically transforms casual smartphone selfies into government-compliant passport, visa, and identity photos for all **204 countries** worldwide. 

It handles automated biometric head measurement (50–69% and 70–80% rules), background removal and replacement (pure solid white, off-white, light blue, light grey), exact aspect ratio cropping, DPI calibration (300/600 DPI), and generation of 4x6 inch printable photo templates.

## Why did I build it?

The origin came from an accident in early 2026: while attempting to buy `photoresizer.in`, I accidentally purchased `photoresizer.us` without a discount coupon. Looking for ways to utilize the `.us` domain, I researched the US photo market and discovered tools like VisaFoto and PhotoGov.

I spent 4–6 months manually researching, analyzing, and verifying government photo regulations directly from official embassy portals, consular directives, and immigration authorities across the globe. I launched a US-focused photo prototype on `photoresizer.us`. Within 20 days, it made its first $100+ in sales. Realizing the massive global demand, I built **VisaReadyNow** using this comprehensive, manually verified international specification dataset.

## The problem

1. **High Cost of Traditional Studios:** Pharmacy and studio photo booths charge $15–$25 per pair of passport photos with inconvenient travel.
2. **Strict Government Rejections:** Visas and passport renewals get delayed for weeks if lighting, head proportions, or background colors deviate even slightly from official guidelines.
3. **Country Inconsistencies:** US requires 2x2 inches (51x51mm) with white background; UK/Schengen require 35x45mm with cream/light grey background; Malaysia requires light blue backgrounds. Standard generic photo editors don't encode these rules.

## The October 2026 60+ Hour Verification Sprint (Oct 5–7, 2026)

Between **October 5 and October 7, 2026**, I executed an intensive **60+ hour sprint** working continuously across 3 days with minimal sleep to bring the platform to enterprise-grade regulatory accuracy:

1. **204 Countries Manually Verified:** Exhaustively audited and verified the passport and visa photo rules for all 204 countries directly against official government embassy portals, consular directives, and foreign affairs immigration ministries.
2. **Official Verified Sources Linked:** Embedded manually verified official government source links into every country specification profile so users and developers can cross-verify regulations directly with primary authorities.
3. **ICAO Passport Photo Dataset:** Created and open-sourced the benchmark dataset [`icao-passport-photo-dataset`](https://github.com/sandeepmaddheshiya/icao-passport-photo-dataset) on GitHub for training and validating compliance engines against international civil aviation biometric standards.
4. **Enhanced Core CV/AI Backend Engine:** Re-calibrated and trained the Python/OpenCV biometric engine using the ICAO dataset and verified country rules for ultra-precise landmark detection, millimeter-to-DPI conversions, and shadow-free segmentation.
5. **Updated PyPI Specification Release:** Published the latest v1.0.x spec package to [PyPI (`visareadynow-passport-specs`)](https://pypi.org/project/visareadynow-passport-specs/) and [npm (`visareadynow-passport-specs`)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/visareadynow-passport-specs).
6. **Pushed Live to Production:** Following rigorous end-to-end testing across 400+ document combinations (passports, tourist visas, business visas, work permits), deployed all updates live to production on `visareadynow.com`.

## What happened

## What went wrong

- **Payment Gateway Misconfiguration (The April Loss):** On April 4–5, 2026, early users attempted to purchase photos on the US prototype, but transactions failed because international payment acceptance was disabled in Razorpay. After resolving the issue with Razorpay support on April 6, the first successful payment came through.
- **Part-Time Bandwidth Bottleneck:** Trying to juggle enterprise SDE workload with high-availability micro-SaaS support caused ranking drops in May–June before going full-time in July 2026.

## What I learned

1. **Real Willingness to Pay:** Users willingly pay $5–$10 for an instant compliance guarantee when applying for critical visas compared to free generic image resizers.
2. **Primary Sources Are Invaluable:** Scraping third-party aggregators introduces errors (e.g., outdated head percentage rules). Manually verifying primary consular directives and publishing open-source benchmarks creates an unassailable data moat.
3. **Payment Gateway Readiness:** Always verify international currency settlement, 3D Secure fallback, and webhooks in sandbox and live mode before sending traffic to checkout flows.
