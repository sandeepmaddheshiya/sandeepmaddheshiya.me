## What is it?

**ZyoraAI Interior Design** ([zyoraai.com](https://zyoraai.com)) is an AI-powered room redesign platform available on both the web and as a native Android app on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zyoraai.app&hl=en_IN).

Users upload photos of their current room or interior space, select design themes (e.g. Modern, Scandinavian, Industrial, Minimalist), and receive photorealistic redesigned room concepts.

## Origin & Pivot

Following the retirement of the initial AI resume builder on `zyoraai.com`, I repurposed the domain to explore computer vision and generative diffusion models applied to interior architecture. 

To expand beyond web discovery, I purchased a new **Google Play Developer Console** account, built a companion Android app, and published it directly to the Google Play Store ([com.zyoraai.app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zyoraai.app&hl=en_IN)).

## Current Metrics & Signals

- **Web Traffic:** 1–4 unique visitors per day.
- **Free Trial Conversions:** 15–20 users have registered for the free trial.
- **Android App Installs:** 10+ active installs on the Google Play Store.
- **Paid Revenue:** **$0** so far. While trial signups occur, free users have not yet converted to paying customers.

## Current Experiments

1. **App Store Optimization (ASO):** Optimizing the Google Play Store listing title, screenshot sequence, localization, and keyword metadata to capture high-intent room redesign searches.
2. **On-Page & Off-Page Web SEO:** Testing informational search clusters (e.g. interior restyling ideas, room layout inspiration) to drive top-of-funnel organic web traffic.
3. **UI/UX Onboarding:** Streamlining the generation workflow to demonstrate high visual value before prompting for paywalls or subscriptions.
