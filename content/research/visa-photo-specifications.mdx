## Summary of Findings

Automating visa and passport photo compliance requires handling strict government guidelines that vary significantly by country:

- **US / India / China:** 2x2 inch (51x51mm) square photos with head height between 50% and 69% of total image height.
- **Schengen Area / UK / Canada:** 35x45mm rectangular format with strict off-white / light grey background requirements.
- **Background Uniformity:** Automated removal requires preserving natural edge feathering around hair while replacing shadows with pure solid RGB values.

## Architectural Decision

Offload background removal and face landmark detection to a Python/FastAPI microservice utilizing OpenCV and lightweight segmentation models, returning processed images directly to Next.js clients.
