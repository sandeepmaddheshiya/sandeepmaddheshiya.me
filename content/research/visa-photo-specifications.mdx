## Summary of Findings

Automating visa and passport photo compliance requires handling strict government regulations that vary significantly across all 204 international jurisdictions. Every single specification across all 204 countries was manually researched, cross-referenced, and verified directly from official government embassy portals, consular directives, and immigration guidelines across two major research phases (April 2026 and an intensive 60+ hour sprint in October 2026):

- **US / India / China / Colombia:** 2x2 inch (51x51mm) square photos with head height strictly between 50% and 69% of total image height.
- **Schengen Area / UK / Canada / Australia:** 35x45mm rectangular format with strict off-white / light grey background requirements (cream for UK) and 70–80% face coverage.
- **Malaysia & Specialized Consulates:** 35x50mm with solid light blue background.
- **Background Uniformity & Lighting:** Automated segmentation requires preserving natural edge feathering around hair while eliminating harsh shadows and replacing backgrounds with exact target RGB hex codes.
- **DPI & EXIF Metadata Standards:** Official upload portals reject uncalibrated uploads if EXIF density headers deviate from required 300 DPI or 600 DPI standards.
- **Official Government Links:** All 204 country profiles now include verified direct links to the official government authority documentation.

## Core Engine Training & Production Deployment

Using the ICAO dataset and verified 204-country rule matrix, the backend Python/OpenCV biometric engine was calibrated and tested against 400+ document configurations. The enhanced compliance engine is now live in production on [VisaReadyNow](/projects/visareadynow).
