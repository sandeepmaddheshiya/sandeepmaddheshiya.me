## The Regulatory Jungle

Most developers assume a passport photo is simply a "square image with a white background." 

When researching visa and passport compliance for [VisaReadyNow](/projects/visareadynow), I manually examined and verified immigration regulations across all **204 countries** directly from primary consular directives and ICAO standards. The reality is wildly fragmented:

- **United States:** Strict 2x2 inch (51x51mm) square format. The head (from bottom of chin to top of hair) must be between 50% and 69% of the overall image height (1 to 1 3/8 inches). Background must be pure solid white or off-white.
- **Schengen Area (EU) / UK / Canada:** 35x45mm rectangular format. The head must occupy 70–80% of the vertical frame (32–36mm). The background must be light grey or cream—never pure stark white (which causes edge blending on biometric scanners).
- **Malaysia:** 35x50mm with a solid light blue background.
- **DPI Metadata:** File uploads are rejected by consular validation systems if the EXIF DPI header is missing or uncalibrated to 300/600 DPI.

## Extracting the Rules into Open-Source Code

Rather than hardcoding conditional statements throughout our application codebase, I normalized all national requirements into a typed specification schema and benchmark dataset:
- **ICAO Dataset:** [`icao-passport-photo-dataset`](https://github.com/sandeepmaddheshiya/icao-passport-photo-dataset)
- **npm (TypeScript):** [`visareadynow-passport-specs`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/visareadynow-passport-specs)
- **PyPI (Python):** [`visareadynow-passport-specs`](https://pypi.org/project/visareadynow-passport-specs/)

```typescript
export interface CountryPassportSpec {
  countryCode: string;
  countryName: string;
  widthMm: number;
  heightMm: number;
  minHeadHeightPercent: number;
  maxHeadHeightPercent: number;
  backgroundColor: 'white' | 'light-grey' | 'light-blue' | 'off-white';
  recommendedDpi: number;
  printableGrid: {
    sheetSize: '4x6' | 'A4';
    photosPerSheet: number;
  };
}
```

## The Facial Landmark Pipeline

1. **Detection & Bounding Box:** Detect face bounding coordinates, chin base, and top-of-skull landmarks.
2. **Dynamic Scaling:** Calculate the required crop bounds such that `(chin - topOfHead) / totalHeight` falls strictly within the target country's statutory percentage range.
3. **Background Replacement:** Apply semantic segmentation to separate hair, ears, and shoulders, blending edges onto the country's exact RGB specification.
4. **Printable Sheet Assembly:** Automatically arrange 4 or 6 copies on a 4x6 inch canvas with precise cut lines for pharmacy printing booths.
